# AMI evaluation subset

Fixed 60–240 s crops of 3 headset-mix recordings; 0 development, 3 speaker-disjoint holdout. English AMI meetings with unscripted speech; the corpus includes scenario and non-scenario meetings. Not Russian accuracy evidence.

Audio: AMI Meeting Corpus, https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/overview.shtml ; Creative Commons Attribution, attribution to AMI project/Carletta et al. (2006). Edinburgh dataset card identifies CC-BY-4.0: https://huggingface.co/datasets/edinburghcstr/ami .

Reference: pyannote/BUTSpeechFIT AMI-diarization-setup, only_words, derived from manual annotations 1.6.2. Word timing uses forced alignment; not newly hand-timed gold. Setup license/README included. Exact commit, URLs and SHA-256 in manifest.json. No reference is generated by our diarizer. Short-reference-interval metrics include word fragments separated by annotated pauses, not only conversational backchannels.
